## Chapter Notes

**Severity:** WARNING
**Trigger:** `vault_check.py chapters` gives an ASK row: one chapter
holds two or more `type: chapter` notes.

**Procedure:** read both notes and show the GM what each holds
that the other does not (fields, then body). Never merge or pick on
your own. Propose, on the GM's word:

- Keep the note named like its folder, unless the GM prefers the
  other.
- Move the other note's unique fields and body into the kept one.
- Delete the other note, or, if the GM keeps it, give it another type
  (such as `document`) and a link to the kept note. A note with no type
  lands in the index's Stubs.

**Not flagged:** notes in any top-level `_` folder.
