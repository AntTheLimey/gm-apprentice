# Call of Cthulhu 7e -- Weapons Reference

Melee, missile, and firearm tables with damage, range, and
the 7e skill each weapon uses. Used during character creation
(equipping investigators) and play (damage lookups during
combat). Damage values come from Basic Roleplaying (ORC); the
skills, base chances, and rules around them are 7e. The Keeper
Rulebook's own weapon list is Table XVII (pp.401-405).

## Reading the Tables

- **Skill**: The 7e Fighting, Firearms, or Throw specialization
  (Keeper Rulebook p.64). Clubs, knives, hatchets, and other
  weapons anyone could pick up use Fighting (Brawl) (p.104).
  Hammers, maces, and staves have no specialization of their
  own; Brawl is the nearest, and the Keeper may ask for another.
  Slings and blowguns fit no 7e specialization; Throw is the
  nearest.
- **Base**: The 7e base chance of that skill.
- **Dmg**: Damage rolled. "+DB" = add the full damage bonus.
  "+½DB" = add half the damage bonus: thrown weapons and
  missiles the user's strength drives (bows, slings), never
  crossbows (p.108).
- **Attk**: Attacks per round. Fractions (1/2, 1/3) mean one
  attack every 2 or 3 rounds.
- **Impale**: Whether the weapon impales on an Extreme success.
- **Rng**: Base range. Long range (up to 2x base) needs a
  Hard success; very long range (up to 4x base) an Extreme
  success.
- **Hands**: 1H = one-handed, 2H = two-handed.

## Extreme Successes and Impales

7e has one rule where Basic Roleplaying had five special
success types (Keeper Rulebook p.103). An Extreme success on
the attacker's own turn (not when fighting back) deals:

- **Non-impaling weapon** (fist, club, mace): maximum weapon
  damage plus maximum damage bonus.
- **Impaling weapon** (blade, spear, arrow, bullet): the same,
  plus one normal damage roll for the weapon.

A critical (01) also deals maximum damage (p.89). Snaring,
tripping, strangling, and knocking a foe back are fighting
maneuvers (p.105), not damage types: a bola or whip that
entangles, or a shield bash that shoves, is a maneuver with
that weapon's skill. A garrote's victim makes a maneuver to
escape or takes 1D6 a round (p.64).

**Burst and full auto** (Keeper Rulebook p.114, summary p.410):
declare the bullets fired before rolling. Full auto splits into
volleys of skill/10 bullets (never fewer than 3), one attack
roll each; a burst is one volley. The first roll uses the range
difficulty and modifiers; each later roll adds a penalty die (at
a third, stay at two penalty dice and raise the difficulty one
step). A success hits with half the volley (round down, at least
one); an Extreme success hits with all of it, the first half
impaling, unless the shot needed an Extreme success to hit at
all (then nothing impales). Moving between targets spends a
bullet per yard.

## Explosives and Grenades

7e rates a blast by distance rather than radius (Keeper
Rulebook Table III, p.124). For a hand grenade or a stick of
dynamite:

| Distance from the blast | Damage |
|-------------------------|--------|
| Within 3 yards | 4D10 |
| 3 to 6 yards | 2D10 |
| 6 to 9 yards | 1D10 |

Larger charges move up the table; smaller ones down. Letting a
target who sees it coming dive for cover with a Dodge roll is a
Keeper's call, borrowed from the firearms rule (p.113).

| Other devices | Effect |
|---------------|--------|
| Concussion grenade | Stuns rather than kills; Keeper's call on a CON roll |
| Molotov | 1D10 fire (Table III's burning room), sets targets alight |
| Smoke grenade | Obscures vision: a penalty die to shoot through it |
| Gas grenade | As the gas: treat a poison gas as a poison (p.128) |

## Firearm Spot Rules

**Reloading**: 1 round per 2 shells; 1 round to swap a clip.
Machine gun belt change takes 2 rounds.

**Malfunctions**: An attack roll equal to or above the Mal
number means the weapon does not fire (Keeper Rulebook p.118).
Revolvers, break-open guns, and bolt-actions: a dud round.
Lever-actions: a jam, cleared in 1D6 rounds with a Mechanical
Repair or Firearms roll, tried once a round.

**Point-blank range**: Within a fifth of the shooter's DEX in
feet, the attack gains a bonus die (Keeper Rulebook p.113).
Not with rifles, full-length shotguns, or bows; sawn-off and
short-barrelled shotguns do get it (p.127).

**Extended range**: Base range = Regular. Up to 2x base = Hard
(half skill). Up to 4x base = Extreme (a fifth of skill); there
only a critical impales (p.112).

**No damage bonus**: Firearms never apply the attacker's
damage bonus (p.33).
